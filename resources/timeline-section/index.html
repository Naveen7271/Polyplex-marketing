TechNova's Beginnings

Founded in 1971, TechNova introduced India's first micro-grained aluminium offset plates and deep-etch chemicals. Electro-grained and anodised plates, along with wipe-on chemistry, followed, establishing its foundation in print and imaging.

Expanding Possibilities, Going Green

TechNova expanded into textile drafting films, screen-making emulsions and laser plotter media. Its eco-friendly, VOC-free chemicals helped pioneer the Go Green movement in India's print industry.

Polyplex Takes Shape

Polyplex began with a single-line PET film facility in Khatima, producing 4,000 tonnes annually. Within a decade, capacity reached 20,000 tonnes, supported by expansion into PET resin production.

Recognition for GATF

Recognition from innovation marked a milestone in TechNova's commitment to advancing print technology through continuous innovation.

Advancing Digital Print and Building a Global Footprint

TechNova expanded into thermal and violet CTP plates, digital printing systems and inkjet media. Alliances with Konica Minolta, HP Indigo, Esko and MGI strengthened a portfolio serving customers in over 60 countries. Polyplex expanded into Thailand in 2003, Turkey in 2005, the US by 2013 and Indonesia by 2017, bringing film and resin manufacturing closer to markets across Asia, Europe and the Americas.

Introducing SaraPrint

SaraPrint combined print quality, durability and versatility, opening new possibilities for labels, graphics and product communication.

Uniting for a Brighter Future

TechNova and Polyplex formed a joint venture, combining a comprehensive portfolio of print, imaging and film solutions with global manufacturing expertise. Together, they opened new possibilities for innovation, growth and a brighter shared future.
